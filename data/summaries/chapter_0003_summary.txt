In Chapter 0003, Jamie, a police officer, investigates the crime scene of a recent break-in and murder. As he examines the area where the window was broken and the victim's body was found, he grapples with confusion about the circumstances surrounding the incident. While he initially assumes a robbery gone wrong, he notes inconsistencies such as the lack of evidence that a thief would have left, making him suspect there is more to the case. Jamie's detective instincts kick in as he considers the possibility of inside involvement, but his efforts yield little information.

Suddenly, Jamie is startled by a young man named Sirius Wolfe, who appears to be another investigator. Sirius quickly deduces crucial details about the case, revealing his sharp analytical skills. He confirms Jamie's suspicions about the crime scene, stating that it could not be a simple break-in. In a twist, Sirius introduces himself as the private detective assigned to the case, leaving Jamie surprised and somewhat incredulous. This encounter sets the stage for a collaborative investigation, hinting at a complex mystery that Jamie must navigate alongside the young detective.
